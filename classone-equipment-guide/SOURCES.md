## Translation policy

- Descriptions are English translations of the clinic's Korean text.
- Superlatives, claims of being unique, result guarantees, safety absolutes and certification claims are not carried over, so the guide doesn't make advertising claims that may be restricted under the Korean Medical Service Act.
- Icon Laser (아이콘레이저) and Quadessy (쿼드세이) have no description on the clinic page, so none is shown.
